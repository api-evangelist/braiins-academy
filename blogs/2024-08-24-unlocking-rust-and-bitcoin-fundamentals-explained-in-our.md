Understand bitcoin technology by building your own version of it using the programming language Rust. Whether you’re a student, developer, or curious about bitcoin, this guide could be your next big step. This book is more than just theory: You’ll create a CPU miner, design a bitcoin library, build a node and your own command-line wallet through learning and coding in Rust.
